Spider man Tobey Maguire

Spider-Man (2002) is a landmark superhero film directed by Sam Raimi and starring Tobey Maguire as Peter Parker, Willem Dafoe as Norman Osborn (Green Goblin), and Kirsten Dunst as Mary Jane Watson. The story serves as the cinematic origin story for the character, following a timid teenager who gains superhuman abilities after being bitten by a genetically engineered spider. After the tragic death of his uncle, he takes on the mantle of Spider-Man to fight crime across New York City.

Spider-man Andrew Garfield

Andrew Russell Garfield is an acclaimed English and American actor celebrated for his versatile performances spanning independent films, stage productions, and major Hollywood blockbusters. Born in Los Angeles and raised in the United Kingdom, he initially made waves in British television and theater before rising to full international prominence.
